Parts

What the parts do.

Examples of parts people ask us to find. Each line is only what the part does. Not a stock list, and not a part number.

Excavator hydraulic pump
It turns engine power into oil flow that moves the boom, arm and attachments.
Final drive
It reduces travel-motor speed so the tracks or wheels can pull the machine.
Main control valve
It directs hydraulic oil to the function the operator selects.
Truck turbo
It uses exhaust flow to drive a compressor that forces more air into the engine.
Turbo housing
It is the casing that contains the turbo and directs exhaust or intake air through it.
Wheel hub bearing
On Volvo, Renault, DAF and Scania trucks, it lets the wheel turn on the axle while carrying the vehicle’s weight.
Kingpin set
It is the pivot hardware in a steered axle that lets the wheels turn left and right.
Prestige brake caliper
It clamps the pads against the disc when the brake is applied.
Car alternator
It is driven by the engine to charge the battery and feed the car’s electrical system.
